# Purpose

Per `00-Project-Charter.md`'s Design Inspiration section, Inkigo is named as a reference point for this product's quality bar. This document is the place to record what, specifically, this project studies and adapts from Inkigo's design language — never to copy its screens outright.

**Status note:** this document is currently a review framework, not a completed analysis — the specific observations below must be filled in and verified by the design team through direct, current hands-on use of the app, since design details evolve between app versions and should not be asserted here from memory or assumption.

---

# What to Evaluate

## Onboarding & First-Run Experience

- How many steps to a first meaningful action?
- Relevant comparison point: Journey 1 (`requirements/User-Journeys.md`).

## Core Loop Speed

- How many taps from opening the app to marking something complete?
- Relevant comparison point: "Core Loop Is Sacred" (`design/UX-Principles.md`).

## Visual Hierarchy & Typography

- Type scale restraint vs. variety on the primary screen.
- Relevant comparison point: `design/Typography.md`, `design/Spacing.md`.

## Color Usage

- Palette restraint, and how per-habit identity (if any) is expressed visually.
- Relevant comparison point: `design/Colors.md`.

## Motion & Feedback

- What animates, how long, and how restrained the "delight" moments are.
- Relevant comparison point: `design/Motion.md`.

## Consistency & History Handling

- How the app visualizes streaks/consistency over time, and whether it leans toward gamification or calm data presentation.
- Relevant comparison point: `design/Components.md`'s StreakBadge/CalendarHeatmap, and the Charter's stance against gamification beyond streaks.

## What Feels Overbuilt or Cluttered

- Note anything that feels like feature creep or visual noise relative to this project's minimalism goals — explicitly not to be adopted.
